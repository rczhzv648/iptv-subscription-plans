## Which IPTV subscription plan makes sense for one viewer?

For one person watching on one screen at a time, the **1-connection plan** is the obvious starting point.

The math is also pretty easy:

* **1 month:** $15 total, or $15/month
* **3 months:** $40 total, or about $13.33/month
* **6 months:** $60 total, or $10/month
* **12 months:** $100 total, or about $8.33/month

The annual option is therefore about **44% cheaper per month** than paying the $15 monthly rate twelve times. The six-month option is about **33% cheaper per month**, while three months works out to roughly **11% less per month** than the monthly plan.

That makes the real decision less about features and more about commitment. You are not paying extra for a “premium” channel tier; you are mainly paying for a longer billing period.
